Our History

Shaishav Public School was established on 8th April 2019 with a vision to provide quality education that goes beyond textbooks. Since then, the school has grown into a caring learning community nurturing 300 students with 12 dedicated teaching staff and 06 non-teaching staff members.

Over the past two decades, we have consistently maintained our commitment to academic excellence, character building, and the holistic development of every child who walks through our doors.

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by purpose, driven by passion

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating and inclusive learning environment that empowers students to achieve their highest potential through innovative pedagogy, strong values, and a global perspective.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence that inspires lifelong learning, cultivates responsible citizenship, and nurtures future leaders who contribute meaningfully to society and the world.

Our Core Values

The pillars that define who we are

Integrity

We uphold honesty, transparency, and ethical behaviour in everything we do.

Innovation

We embrace creativity and encourage out-of-the-box thinking at every level.

Inclusivity

We celebrate diversity and ensure every child feels valued and respected.

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in academics, sports, and character development.

Sustainability

We foster environmental awareness and responsible stewardship of our planet.

Compassion

We nurture empathy and encourage students to serve their communities with kindness.
