Our History

Shaishav Public School was established on 8th April 2019 with a vision to provide quality education that goes beyond textbooks. Since then, the school has grown into a caring learning community nurturing 300 students with 12 dedicated teaching staff and 06 non-teaching staff members.

From the Principal's Desk

Principal, Shaishav Public School

Dear Parents and Students,

At Shaishav Public School, we believe that true education is the harmonious blending of Indian tradition with scientific temperament. Our ancient heritage has gifted us timeless values — discipline, respect, compassion, and the pursuit of knowledge — that remain as relevant today as they were centuries ago. At the same time, we recognise that the future belongs to those who think critically, question boldly, and innovate fearlessly.

Our educational philosophy is built on four pillars — Health, Service, Values, and Self-study — each deeply rooted in Indian wisdom yet perfectly aligned with the demands of the modern world. We nurture children who can recite a shloka with the same confidence with which they conduct a science experiment, who understand the dignity of service alongside the power of technology.

We do not merely teach subjects; we shape personalities. Every child at Shaishav is encouraged to embrace the richness of Indian culture — through yoga, moral education, and community service — while simultaneously developing a rational, scientific outlook towards life. This balance, we believe, creates well-rounded individuals who are proud of their roots and ready for the world.

I invite you to be a part of this beautiful journey where tradition meets progress, and where every child's potential is nurtured with love, care, and purpose.

"Education is that which liberates." — We strive to liberate young minds through the light of knowledge, the warmth of Indian values, and the power of scientific thinking.

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by purpose, driven by passion

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating and inclusive learning environment that empowers students to achieve their highest potential through innovative pedagogy, strong values, and a global perspective.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence that inspires lifelong learning, cultivates responsible citizenship, and nurtures future leaders who contribute meaningfully to society and the world.
