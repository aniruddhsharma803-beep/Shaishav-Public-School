CBSE Curriculum

A rigorous yet flexible curriculum designed to nurture intellectual curiosity and critical thinking

Primary School (Classes I–V)

Our primary programme builds a strong foundation in literacy, numeracy, and social skills. Learning is activity-based, joyful, and aligned with the National Education Policy 2020.

Each child receives individual attention with a student-to-teacher ratio of 25:1, ensuring no one is left behind.

Middle School (Classes VI–VIII)

The middle school years focus on deepening conceptual understanding and developing analytical thinking. Students are introduced to formal science disciplines and begin exploring their interests through a variety of electives.

Project-based learning, interdisciplinary approaches, and technology integration make education come alive.

Secondary School (Classes IX–X)

The secondary section prepares students for the CBSE Board Examinations. With focused exam preparation, regular assessments, and remedial classes, we ensure every student achieves their personal best.

Career counselling sessions help students make informed choices about their future academic streams.

🌐 Social Science
💻 Information Technology
🎨 Art Education (Optional)

Senior Secondary (Classes XI–XII)

The senior secondary programme offers three streams — Science, Commerce, and Humanities — each designed to provide deep specialization while preparing students for competitive examinations and university admissions.

Dedicated laboratories, library resources, and mentorship programmes support academic growth at this critical stage.
