Beyond the Classroom

At Shaishav, we believe that true education extends far beyond textbooks. Our diverse range of extracurricular activities ensures every student discovers their passion.

Sports & Athletics

Cricket, football, basketball, badminton, swimming, athletics, and more. Our students compete at district, state, and national levels, guided by professional coaches.

Music & Dance

Classical and western music, folk and contemporary dance forms. Students perform at inter-school festivals and our grand Annual Day celebration.

Art & Craft

Drawing, painting, pottery, and crafts. Our dedicated art studio provides a creative space where imagination takes shape.

Eco Club

Tree plantation drives, waste management workshops, and environmental awareness campaigns foster responsible global citizens.

Drama & Theatre

Students explore storytelling, character building, and teamwork through plays, skits, and inter-school drama competitions.

Community Service

Blood donation camps, charity events, old-age home visits, and rural school outreach programmes instil empathy and social responsibility.

Science Club

Science fairs, astronomy nights, innovation challenges, and lab experiments that spark curiosity and scientific inquiry.

Every Child Has a Hidden Talent

At Shaishav, we help them find it. Explore our activities and give your child a platform to shine.
