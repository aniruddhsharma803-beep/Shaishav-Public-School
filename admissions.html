Admission Process

A simple, transparent, step-by-step admission journey

Step 1 — Enquiry & Registration

Visit our campus or fill out the online enquiry form. Collect the admission prospectus and registration form from the school office or download it from our website.

Step 2 — Submit Application

Complete the registration form with all required documents (birth certificate, previous report cards, transfer certificate, passport-size photos, Aadhaar copy) and submit along with the registration fee.

Step 3 — Entrance Assessment

Students applying for Class I and above will appear for an age-appropriate written assessment and an informal interaction with faculty members.

Step 4 — Parent Interaction

A brief interaction with parents helps us understand the family's expectations and share our educational philosophy and school policies.

Step 5 — Admission Offer

Successful candidates receive an admission offer letter. Confirm the seat by paying the admission fee within the specified deadline.

Step 6 — Welcome to Shaishav!

Collect the student kit, uniform, and bus pass. Attend the orientation programme and begin your exciting journey at Shaishav Public School!

Documents Required

Please keep the following documents ready before applying

Birth Certificate

Original and one photocopy of the child's birth certificate.

Aadhaar Card

Aadhaar cards of the student and both parents (photocopy).

Previous Report Card

Last two years' report cards from the previous school.

Transfer Certificate

Original TC from the previous school (for Class II onwards).

Passport Photos

4 recent passport-size photographs of the student.

Address Proof

Any government-issued address proof (utility bill, ration card, etc.).
